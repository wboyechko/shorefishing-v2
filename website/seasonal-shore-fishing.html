Learn How To Fish

Seasonal Shore Fishing

Learn how weather, light, moon phases and changing conditions can help you decide when to head to the shoreline.

When Should You Go Fishing?

The simplest answer from the original ShoreFishing.net guide is: any time you can.

Fish feed all the time, but there are certain conditions that may improve your chances of having a successful shore fishing trip.

Watch The Conditions

Instead of relying on one rule, pay attention to changes in weather, light, water and the time of day.

01

Weather Changes

The original guide suggests that the period right before or after a change in air pressure can be a good time to head to the water.

It specifically mentions the period before or after rain as a worthwhile time to try.

Moon Phases

The original guide notes that many experienced fishermen pay attention to moon phases.

Some anglers mentioned in the original material favor the three days before, during and three days after a new moon.

Others favor the same period around a full moon.

A Starting Point

Treat moon phases as something to observe and compare with your own fishing experience, rather than a guarantee of success.

04

Night Fishing

Night fishing can be an exciting way to experience the shoreline.

The original guide recommends carrying a flashlight or headlamp.

Safety First

If you build a fire, check the local fire regulations and make absolutely sure the fire is completely out before leaving the fishing spot.

06

Clear & Calm Conditions

Clear, calm days can still produce fish.

However, the original guide observes that the amount of fish may not be as good as when there are some ripples on the water.

07

Put The Conditions Together

Don't rely on one condition by itself. Look at the overall situation before deciding where and when to fish.

Weather Is a weather change approaching or has one just passed?
Light Is it dawn, dusk or another low-light period?
Moon Is there a moon phase that you want to observe and compare?
Water Is there a breeze creating movement on the water?
Location Does the shoreline offer structure or a feeding area?
Experience What have you learned from fishing this location before?

Keep A Fishing Log

The original guide encourages experimentation: different locations, different lures and different times of day or night.

One of the best ways to learn is to remember what the conditions were when you caught fish.

Your Experience Matters

Over time, your own observations can become one of the most useful tools in deciding when to return to a location.

There Is No Perfect Time

Weather changes. Water changes. Light changes. Fish change.

The goal isn't to wait for a perfect day. It's to understand the conditions, make an informed choice and get out on the shoreline whenever you can.

That's the spirit of the original ShoreFishing.net advice.
